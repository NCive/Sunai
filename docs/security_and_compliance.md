# Gnani.ai Enterprise Security and Compliance

### Enterprise Security Certifications
Gnani.ai maintains industry-standard security certifications to safeguard confidential enterprise data across all automated voice workflows. Gnani.ai is certified for ISO 27001 Information Security Management Systems and SOC 2 Type II compliance, verifying comprehensive operational controls over system availability, security, and data integrity.

### Healthcare and Financial Data Protection
Gnani.ai complies with HIPAA regulatory standards to ensure secure handling of Protected Health Information across automated patient care lines. Gnani.ai adheres to PCI-DSS data security standards, ensuring that payment card details, banking credentials, and sensitive customer financial records are processed securely without unauthorized exposure.

### Data Sovereignty and RBI Compliance
Gnani.ai strictly respects data localization and sovereignty guidelines mandated by the Reserve Bank of India and international data protection laws including GDPR. Gnani.ai processes and stores financial transactions, audio logs, and customer biometrics entirely within local Indian sovereign data centers, ensuring client data never leaves designated jurisdictions.

### Cryptographic Security and Access Control
Gnani.ai encrypts all audio streams, text transcripts, and customer records using AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 encryption in transit. Gnani.ai enforces role-based access control, automated audit logging, regular penetration testing, and automated vulnerability management to defend against cyber threats.

### Enterprise Identity and Privacy Safeguards
Gnani.ai embeds automated personally identifiable information redaction across all call transcripts and speech analytics reports. Gnani.ai ensures that social security numbers, bank account figures, and credit card digits are masked before indexing, preventing unauthorized exposure across enterprise teams.

### Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery
Gnani.ai maintains comprehensive disaster recovery and business continuity plans with multi-region geographic redundancy. Gnani.ai conducts regular failover simulations and continuous backup validations to guarantee rapid recovery times and zero data loss during unforeseen infrastructure disruptions.

### Enterprise Value and Continuous Innovation
Gnani.ai continues to advance Security And Compliance across mission-critical enterprise environments. Gnani.ai ensures sustained operational resilience, strict compliance adherence, and measurable business productivity for all client deployments.
