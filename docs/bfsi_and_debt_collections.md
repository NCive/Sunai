# Gnani.ai BFSI Solutions and Agentic Debt Collections

### Banking and Insurance Transformation
Gnani.ai provides specialized conversational artificial intelligence solutions tailored for the Banking, Financial Services, and Insurance (BFSI) industry. Gnani.ai automates routine banking transactions, loan application inquiries, policy renewal notifications, KYC identity verifications, and claims intake workflows across major financial institutions.

### Enterprise BFSI Clients: TVS Credit and Bajaj Allianz
TVS Credit and Bajaj Allianz were among the earliest enterprise institutions to deploy Gnani.ai's conversational voice engines. TVS Credit utilizes Gnani.ai voice bots to scale lending operations and automate borrower payment reminders, while Bajaj Allianz deploys Gnani.ai to manage high-volume multilingual insurance policyholder inquiries and renewal verifications.

### Agentic Debt Collections Automation
Gnani.ai automates pre-due and post-due loan collections with empathetic, multilingual voice AI agents. Gnani.ai collections agents engage borrowers in their preferred regional dialect, negotiate manageable payment plans, verify settlement eligibility, and capture binding promise-to-pay commitments in accordance with enterprise lending guidelines.

### In-Call Payment Completion with Razorpay
Gnani.ai partnered with Razorpay to pioneer an agentic voice collections platform featuring native in-call payment completion. Gnani.ai voice bots integrate with Razorpay's Model Context Protocol (MCP) server, allowing borrowers to authorize and complete secure UPI and card payments directly within the live phone call without switching to external web links or text messages.

### Regulatory Compliance and Borrower Dignity
Gnani.ai enforces strict adherence to Reserve Bank of India debt recovery fair practice codes and borrower privacy mandates. Gnani.ai collections agents maintain respectful conversational tone, prevent harassing calling frequencies, and generate complete audio and transcript audit logs for enterprise compliance reviews.

### Measurable Recovery Performance and Business Value
Gnani.ai increases debt recovery rates by up to thirty percent while reducing collection operating costs by more than fifty percent. Gnani.ai provides continuous performance reporting and delinquency tracking, enabling risk management leaders to optimize credit portfolios at scale.
