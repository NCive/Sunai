# Gnani.ai Leadership and Investors

### Executive Leadership and Co-Founders
Gnani.ai was co-founded by Ganesh Gopalan and Ananth Nagaraj in 2016 in Bengaluru, India. Ganesh Gopalan serves as the Chief Executive Officer of Gnani.ai, steering global commercial strategy, enterprise sales, client partnerships, and corporate vision. Ananth Nagaraj serves as the Chief Technology Officer of Gnani.ai, directing core artificial intelligence research, speech model engineering, neural network training, and platform scalability.

### Early Strategic Backing from Samsung Ventures
Gnani.ai secured early venture capital backing from Samsung Venture Investment (Samsung Ventures) in 2019. Samsung Ventures invested in Gnani.ai to support the development of high-accuracy speech recognition algorithms and local Indic language processing for mobile devices, smart consumer electronics, and connected appliances across emerging markets.

### Series A Investment by Info Edge Ventures
Gnani.ai completed a Series A funding round of four million dollars in July 2024, led by Info Edge Ventures. Info Edge Ventures backed Gnani.ai to accelerate sales expansion across Indian enterprise markets, scale engineering teams, and expand automated voice agent capabilities in high-volume customer contact centers.

### Series B Growth Round Led by Aavishkaar Capital
Gnani.ai secured a Series B funding round of ten million dollars in March 2026 led by Aavishkaar Capital, with continued participation from existing investor Info Edge Ventures. Gnani.ai utilizes this Series B growth capital to expand international operations, scale sovereign foundational voice-to-voice models such as Inya VoiceOS, and enhance enterprise customer support systems globally.

### Capital Efficiency and Enterprise Milestones
Gnani.ai has raised over fourteen million dollars in total institutional venture funding while achieving operational profitability across key business lines. Gnani.ai demonstrates sustainable capital efficiency by maintaining in-house research facilities, direct enterprise sales channels, and long-term recurring SaaS relationships with premier financial institutions.
