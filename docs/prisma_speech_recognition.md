# Gnani Prisma Speech-to-Text Recognition Engine

### Automatic Speech Recognition Overview
Gnani Prisma is Gnani.ai's proprietary Automatic Speech Recognition engine engineered specifically for conversational Indian and global multilingual speech. Gnani Prisma delivers high-accuracy speech-to-text conversion across both real-time streaming WebSocket connections and high-volume asynchronous batch audio processing pipelines.

### Multilingual Model Architecture and Prisma v3.0
Prisma v3.0 is a unified multilingual deep learning foundation model supporting ten core Indian languages along with Indian English. Prisma v3.0 provides automatic language identification using the indic-auto configuration, dynamic language candidate hints, and custom domain keyword boosting to achieve industry-leading recognition accuracy across complex enterprise terminology.

### Code-Switching and Dialect Robustness
Prisma v3.0 excels at recognizing code-mixed and conversational multilingual speech, including Hinglish, Tanglish, and Kanglish. Prisma v3.0 handles spontaneous conversational phenomena, colloquial contractions, local regional phrasing, and varying acoustic environments without degradation in word error rate.

### Acoustic Noise Robustness and Telephony Optimization
Gnani Prisma performs robustly across diverse acoustic conditions, including noisy street backgrounds, vehicle interiors, and bandwidth-constrained eight-kilohertz telephony lines. Gnani Prisma features low server-side latency, streaming partial transcripts, and configurable output formats including verbatim, transcribed, and transliterated text.

### Domain Vocabulary Adaptation
Gnani Prisma supports custom domain vocabulary injection, allowing enterprises in banking, healthcare, and insurance to boost recognition accuracy for specific brand names, acronyms, and alphanumeric identifiers. Gnani Prisma easily transcribes policy numbers, medical terms, and specialized financial instruments with minimal error.

### Ultra-Low Latency Streaming Performance
Gnani Prisma delivers partial speech transcripts with roundtrip network latencies under two hundred and fifty milliseconds. Gnani Prisma empowers real-time conversational voice bots to process customer intent fluidly, enabling prompt system responses and natural dialogue cadence without noticeable pauses.

### Enterprise Value and Continuous Innovation
Gnani.ai continues to advance Prisma Speech Recognition across mission-critical enterprise environments. Gnani.ai ensures sustained operational resilience, strict compliance adherence, and measurable business productivity for all client deployments.
