# Inya Insights Omnichannel Analytics and QA

### Automated Quality Assurance Platform
Inya Insights, previously known as Aura365, is Gnani.ai's enterprise speech analytics and automated Quality Assurance platform. Inya Insights transcribes and evaluates one hundred percent of omnichannel customer conversations, replacing traditional manual spot-checking that typically audits only one to two percent of contact center recordings.

### Agent Performance and Sentiment Scoring
Inya Insights scores contact center interactions automatically across customizable performance dimensions, including customer satisfaction, empathy, professional etiquette, and issue resolution completeness. Inya Insights identifies customer sentiment trends and agent behavioral patterns to deliver data-driven coaching recommendations.

### Regulatory Compliance and Risk Monitoring
Inya Insights automatically flags compliance violations, missing regulatory disclaimers, abusive language, and unauthorized disclosures across audio recordings and text transcripts. Inya Insights generates instant audit trails and risk alerts, enabling enterprise compliance teams to resolve potential regulatory violations before escalation.

### Business Intelligence and Customer Insights
Inya Insights converts raw unstructured voice conversations into structured business intelligence dashboards. Inya Insights detects emerging product defects, billing disputes, competitive mentions, and customer churn drivers across millions of customer interactions in real time.

### Root-Cause Analysis and Operational Impact
Inya Insights performs automated root-cause analysis on customer escalations and repeat call drivers across enterprise departments. Inya Insights empowers executive decision-makers with actionable metrics that improve first-contact resolution rates, customer retention, and brand loyalty across distributed support organizations.

### Enterprise Data Integration and Reporting
Inya Insights exports structured call scoring data, sentiment aggregates, and compliance records directly into leading business intelligence platforms like PowerBI and Tableau. Inya Insights allows enterprise leaders to visualize contact center trends alongside corporate revenue metrics seamlessly.

### Enterprise Value and Continuous Innovation
Gnani.ai continues to advance Inya Insights across mission-critical enterprise environments. Gnani.ai ensures sustained operational resilience, strict compliance adherence, and measurable business productivity for all client deployments.
