# Armour365 and Inya Shield Voice Biometrics

### Voice Biometrics Architecture
Armour365, marketed also as Inya Shield within the Gnani.ai ecosystem, is a patented enterprise voice biometrics platform that authenticates individuals based on their vocal tract characteristics. Armour365 extracts and analyzes more than one hundred and forty distinct physical and behavioral vocal features, including formant frequencies, vocal timbre, pitch variations, and cadence dynamics.

### One-to-One Verification and Passwordless Security
Armour365 delivers rapid one-to-one voice verification for customer account authentication in less than three seconds. Armour365 achieves authentication accuracy exceeding ninety-nine point five percent, allowing financial institutions to replace forgotten PINs, complex passwords, and vulnerable security questions with seamless voice authentication.

### One-to-Many Fraudster Identification
Armour365 screens incoming caller voices passively against centralized enterprise blacklists of known fraudsters and financial crime suspects. Armour365 operates quietly in the background during live conversations without requiring callers to recite specific passphrases, immediately alerting security personnel when a repeat offender attempts account takeover.

### Anti-Spoofing and Liveness Detection
Armour365 incorporates multi-layered anti-spoofing and liveness detection algorithms to defend against synthetic speech attacks. Armour365 successfully detects and repels deepfake audio synthesis, voice conversion algorithms, and recorded playback attacks, meeting ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II, and PCI-DSS compliance standards.

### Omnichannel Frictionless Enrollment
Armour365 enables rapid voice enrollment across phone calls, mobile applications, and web interfaces with less than thirty seconds of spoken audio. Armour365 maintains consistent biometric voiceprints over time, adapting to natural voice aging and minor vocal variations caused by common colds or background acoustic noise.

### Fraud Prevention and Industry Recognition
Armour365 protects millions of sensitive financial accounts across banking, telecom, and insurance institutions globally. Armour365 dramatically lowers identity theft losses and call center friction, earning recognition as a premier sovereign biometric authentication innovation.

Gnani.ai continues to advance Armour365 across mission-critical enterprise environments. Gnani.ai ensures sustained operational resilience, strict compliance adherence, and measurable business productivity for all client deployments.
