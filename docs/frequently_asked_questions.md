# Gnani.ai Frequently Asked Questions

### What core capabilities define Gnani.ai?
Gnani.ai is an enterprise voice-first Generative AI company that builds proprietary speech recognition, text-to-speech, natural language understanding, voice biometrics, and omnichannel automation platforms. Gnani.ai enables enterprises to automate high-volume customer conversations across voice, WhatsApp, chat, and email with human-like, multilingual voice agents.

### Which Indian languages does Gnani.ai support?
Gnani.ai supports fourteen Indic languages including Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali, Malayalam, Punjabi, Odia, Assamese, and Urdu, alongside Indian English. Gnani.ai natively handles colloquial code-switching, regional accents, and mixed-language speech patterns like Hinglish across spoken dialogues.

### How does Gnani.ai ensure enterprise security and regulatory compliance?
Gnani.ai is certified for ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II, complies with HIPAA and PCI-DSS standards, and guarantees local data residency in accordance with Reserve Bank of India mandates. Gnani.ai encrypts data using AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit, and offers on-premises and air-gapped installation options.

### What products comprise the Inya enterprise suite?
Gnani.ai provides the Inya AI suite, which includes Inya Workforce for 24/7 omnichannel customer automation, Inya Assist for real-time contact center agent guidance, Inya Shield for voice biometrics and fraud prevention, Inya Insights for 100 percent call QA analytics, and Inya DIY for no-code bot creation.

### How does Gnani.ai perform speech recognition in noisy environments?
Gnani.ai leverages proprietary deep acoustic models trained on extensive real-world audio datasets containing vehicular noise, street ambiance, and band-limited eight-kilohertz telephony channels. Gnani.ai maintains high word accuracy without dropping conversational context in demanding enterprise environments.

### How do enterprise clients integrate with Gnani.ai?
Gnani.ai offers standard REST APIs, WebSocket streaming endpoints, and pre-built connectors for major CRM systems and contact center telephony platforms. Gnani.ai enables organizations to deploy custom voice automation agents in days with dedicated technical support and onboarding assistance.
