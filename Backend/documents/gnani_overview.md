# Gnani.ai Enterprise Overview

Gnani.ai is a leading conversational artificial intelligence technology company founded in 2016 by Ganesh Gopalan and Ananth Nagaraj. Headquartered in Bengaluru, India, the company specializes in building proprietary deep-tech speech recognition, voice biometrics, and natural language processing solutions.

The primary mission of Gnani.ai is to empower enterprises with human-like voice automation across banking, financial services, insurance, healthcare, and telecommunications sectors. The platform enables automated customer engagement, debt collections, lead qualification, and customer support.

Gnani's proprietary speech models are trained on more than ten thousand hours of diverse regional audio data. The platform supports over fourteen Indian languages alongside global and Indian English accents, processing millions of automated voice conversations every day.

Enterprise deployments of Gnani.ai integrate seamlessly with existing contact center infrastructure such as Cisco, Avaya, and Genesys. Key enterprise benefits include reducing customer service operational costs by up to seventy percent while maintaining average resolution times under two minutes.
