# Armour365 Voice Biometrics System

Armour365 is Gnani's patented voice biometrics engine that authenticates users based on their unique vocal tract acoustic characteristics. The system extracts and evaluates over one hundred and forty distinct physical and behavioural voice features including pitch, formant frequencies, cadence, and harmonic resonances.

The system supports two core biometric modes: one-to-one voice verification and one-to-many voice identification. One-to-one verification authenticates callers within three seconds with an accuracy rate exceeding ninety-nine point five percent, effectively replacing insecure passwords and security questions.

One-to-many identification screens incoming caller voices against centralized blacklists of known fraudsters and repeat attackers. The identification engine operates passively in the background during live agent calls without requiring active cooperation or specific passphrases from the speaker.

Armour365 includes built-in anti-spoofing and liveness detection algorithms. These algorithms detect and neutralize synthetic voices generated by text-to-speech, deepfake audio clips, recorded replay attacks, and voice conversion tools. The platform complies with ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II, and PCI-DSS enterprise security standards.
